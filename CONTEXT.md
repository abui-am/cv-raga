# CVRaga

A personal career record supplies confirmed facts for CVs tailored to individual opportunities.

## Language

**My career**: The candidate's shared record of personal details, career history, and supporting evidence.
_Avoid_: Knowledge, master CV

**Career history**: Confirmed roles, projects, education, skills, and certifications maintained once and reused across CVs.

**Supporting evidence**: Additional factual detail or source material that supports a career entry or the candidate's career generally.

**Source document**: An original import or captured statement retained as provenance for career facts.

**Saved CV**: A document assembled for general use or a particular opportunity, with its own preserved revisions.
